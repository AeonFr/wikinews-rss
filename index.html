Wikipedia news as RSS

RSS feeds built every day from Wikipedia’s current events portals.

  • Spanish feed: topics from Portal:Actualidad on Spanish Wikipedia. Its entries are in Spanish.
  • English feed: entries from Portal:Current events on English Wikipedia. Its entries are in English.

Each feed keeps the language of the Wikipedia it comes from, whatever language this page is shown in.

Copy the link address into your feed reader.

Unofficial project, not affiliated with the Wikimedia Foundation. The text comes from Wikipedia contributors and is available under the CC BY-SA 4.0 license; every entry links to the source article and its page history.
